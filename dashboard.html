What is not measured here

Open rates are deliberately absent. Measuring them needs a tracking pixel, and Gmail proxies and pre-fetches images through its own cache — so a large share of "opens" are Google, not a person. A pixel is also itself a spam signal, which would damage the deliverability this page exists to monitor. Replies and bounces are counted because they are real events; opens would be a number that looks precise and means very little.
